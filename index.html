Indigenous Academic Library Collections Access Service

Acknowledgement of Country

We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the lands on which this academic library operates. We pay respect to Elders past and present, and recognise the continuing connection of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples to Country, culture, knowledge and community.

The Indigenous Collections Portal provides access to selected library collection records, including language resources, oral histories, images, manuscripts, and cultural knowledge materials. Some items are open for general discovery, while others require review before access can be approved.

Access conditions are guided by cultural protocols, privacy requirements, and responsible data use principles. Requests for restricted or culturally sensitive items are reviewed by authorised library staff and community reviewers.

Collection Space

About the Library and Collection Space

The academic library provides access to research materials, cultural records, gallery displays and special collections. Some items may require additional review before access is granted.

The Indigenous Academic Library Collections Portal supports responsible access by combining catalogue information, cultural metadata, access conditions and review workflows in one web application.

Service purpose: The Indigenous Academic Library provides access to collection records, request management, and cultural review processes for Indigenous collection materials.

Access Workflow

How Cultural Access Requests Work

The portal is designed to help users understand whether an item can be viewed immediately or whether a request must be reviewed by authorised staff and cultural advisors.

1

Search

Users search or browse catalogue records and review the access level.

2

Request

Logged-in users submit a reason for requesting access to a restricted item.

3

Review

Library staff and reviewers assess the item metadata and request context.

4

Decision

The access status is updated and the decision is recorded in the system.

Item Overview

Recordings of Meriam Mir Speakers

This catalogue record represents a set of audio recordings connected to Meriam Mir language knowledge, Torres Strait Islander cultural memory and community language revival. The item is part of the Languages of the Torres Strait collection.

Because the recordings may include culturally sensitive language content and community knowledge, access is restricted until a review has been completed. The final access decision may depend on the requester’s purpose, item metadata, cultural protocol notes and relevant community guidance.

Cultural Note: This item may contain culturally sensitive language knowledge. Access is subject to review by authorised library staff and relevant community representatives.

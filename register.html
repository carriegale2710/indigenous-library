Create an Account

Register to submit cultural access requests, track request outcomes and receive instructions for viewing approved materials at the library.

Register for Access

Create your library account

Public users can create an account to request access to restricted collection items and track review outcomes. Staff and reviewer permissions are assigned by the library.

Use an email address that library staff can use for access request updates.

Staff and community reviewer accounts may require approval before protected review areas become available.

Before You Register

Account Access Information

Registration allows users to submit access requests and track decisions for restricted or culturally sensitive collection items.

Public and Research Users

Can browse catalogue records, request access to restricted materials and view request outcomes through their account.

Library Staff Accounts

Staff accounts are used to support request administration, catalogue management and supervised access arrangements at the library.

Reviewer Accounts

Community reviewers and Elders may review culturally sensitive records, add consultation comments and support access decisions.

Access reminder: Approval to view restricted material does not automatically allow copying, photography, publication or redistribution. Conditions are recorded with each decision.

After Registration

Requesting Access to Collection Items

After signing in, users can open an item record, submit an access request and provide a reason for use. Approved requests may include instructions for viewing materials at the physical library under supervised conditions.
